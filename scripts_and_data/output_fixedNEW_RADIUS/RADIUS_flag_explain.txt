RADIUS in SURFMAP:
-rad specify additional radius in angstroms that is added to the radius of every atom before molecular surface is calculated by MSMS. Its clearly visible in generated shell surface files.

Changing this value directly affects the surface geometry:
-larger values generate smoother surface by effectively increasing atomic radius. In result, surface features (bigger patterns / shapes / patches more visible)
-smaller values generate more detailed and realistic surface that better reflect actual atomic geometry. However final surface may appear rougher and contain more local features
